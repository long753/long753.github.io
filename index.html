Robotics Researcher

Hi! I'm Long Chen, currently an undergraduate student at Chongqing University and will join Zhejiang University as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2027. My research interests lie in robotics and embodied intelligence, with a particular focus on intelligent decision-making and control in complex, unstructured environments. My long-term goal is to develop robotic systems that can achieve self-evolvinig through interaction with the real world or high-fidelity simulated environments.
